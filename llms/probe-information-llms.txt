# Probe Information Services (Probe42)

> Probe42 is an Indian company-intelligence and business-data platform covering roughly one million Indian companies. It collects, cleans, and curates data from 743 public-domain sources through a five-step verification process, delivering company profiles, financials, director and charge data, and risk signals to banks, NBFCs, fintechs, and sales teams for onboarding, KYC/due-diligence, credit assessment, and lead intelligence. The Probe42 API lets developers embed this company data directly inside their own applications.
